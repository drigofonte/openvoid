Compositions

Flexible layout systems.

In CUBE CSS, compositions are a high-level macro view — even when applied at component scale. The composition layer creates flexible, component-agnostic layout systems that support as many variants of content as possible.

Compositions don't care what blocks live inside them. They only define spatial relationships: how slots are sized, ordered, and spaced. The atomic pieces that fill those slots are documented in Blocks.

Screen-level and layout-level arrangements. These are the largest structural units — they describe how entire pages and major regions are organised.

Stack

04The most-used composition. Vertical sequencing with a single gap value.
Stack flex-direction:column; gap:N

A vertical sequence of children separated by a single gap from the spacing scale. Child-agnostic — works for page sections, card body rows, meta labels, anything stacked vertically.

gap: 8–10pxDefault: alt pills row, quick chips
gap: 14pxCard body items, composer layers
gap: 18pxBetween cards (left column)
gap: 28pxStage-level sections

One gap value per stack. If two adjacent children need different spacing, split them into separate stacks.

Part 2 · Component Compositions

Component-agnostic layout systems at component scale. These compose blocks into larger UI patterns — they define the spatial relationships inside a component, not what the component looks like.
